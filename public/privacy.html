Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This is a placeholder policy and will be updated before PooPal officially launches.

PooPal ("we", "us") helps people find bathrooms near them. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what control you have over it.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google.
  • Your location. If you allow it, your device's location is used to show where you are on the map. Your location is only used in your browser. We do not send it to our servers or store it.
  • Content you submit. When you add or edit a bathroom (for example its name, address, map position, access details, or door code), we store that information along with the ID of the account that submitted it. Bathroom listings are visible to everyone who uses PooPal.

How we use your information

  • To let you sign in and manage your account.
  • To show bathrooms near you on the map.
  • To display bathroom listings and keep track of who added them.
  • To prevent spam and abuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Services we use

We rely on these third-party services to run PooPal. They only receive the information they need to do their job:

  • Supabase stores our database and handles sign-in.
  • Google provides sign-in.
  • Stadia Maps provides the map images. Your browser requests map images for the area you are viewing, so Stadia Maps can see your IP address and that area.
  • Our hosting provider serves the website and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.

Cookies

We use cookies only to keep you signed in. We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account. You can ask us to delete your account at any time by contacting us at the email below. When your account is deleted, bathrooms you added stay on the map but are no longer linked to you.

Children

PooPal is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
